Prom autopilot

Prom autopilot is a private, internal tool of the Vision Dynamics auto parts store on Prom.ua. It prepares product cards (titles, descriptions, search phrases) for the store's own catalogue.

It uses the Google Ads API (Keyword Planner historical metrics) in read-only mode, only for the owner's own Google Ads account, to learn how often search phrases are used in Ukraine and to order them in the store's product cards. It does not create or change ads, campaigns or budgets, and it has no other users.
